desktop tool created and operated by the owner of the TikTok account

## 3. How the Application works

1. The Operator authorizes the Application through the platform's official
   OAuth flow. The Application requests only the permissions it needs:
   reading the basic profile of the connected account, uploading a video and
   publishing a post.
2. The Operator prepares a video and its caption, hashtags, cover image and
   privacy setting on their own computer.
3. Before every post, the Application shows the Operator exactly what will be
   published and waits for an explicit confirmation. Nothing is posted without
   that confirmation.
4. The Application then uploads the video and creates the post on the
   Operator's account using the platform's API.

The Application never posts to accounts other than the Operator's, never posts
in bulk, and never posts content the Operator has not reviewed.
